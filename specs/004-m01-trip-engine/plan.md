# Implementation Plan: Trip Engine (M01)

**Branch**: `004-m01-trip-engine` | **Date**: 2026-10-20 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/004-m01-trip-engine/spec.md`

## Summary

M01 creates `packages/trip_engine`, the pure Dart package that holds every rule deciding a trip's
outcome (constitution I). It covers:

- **The rules:** fares, the tray and the hand, the passing chain, the 12 MVP passenger types, the 8
  authored events, the player aids, reputation, stars and earnings.
- **A functional core:** a caller-driven 33 ms clock, a seeded `xorshift32` used only for speech
  variants, and integer-only arithmetic. Replaying `(definition, seed, params, command_log)`
  reproduces the result, the event sequence and the command-log fingerprint (R2 to R4, R9).
- **Resume after a kill** (mobile §24.4, resolved): the replay record is the checkpoint, and the
  engine rebuilds a trip from it exactly, paused (R10).
- **The first parity fixtures** in `fixtures/parity/`, with a fixture tool and a coverage test (R11).
- **The contracts the rest of the repo needs:**
  - a rules reference for B05's mirror (`packages/trip_engine/RULES.md`);
  - `content/engine/capabilities.json`, with the supported archetypes, events, event parameters and
    speech slots;
  - four content validation rules (V-T8 to V-T11);
  - the `no_fights_required` trip rule, through B04's schema, codegen and authoring app;
  - 20 new tunables, and the hints key turned into a three-value mode (R12, settling M03's
    hand-over).

Technical approach and versions: [research.md](./research.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**:

- Dart 3.13.5, bundled with the FVM-pinned Flutter 3.47.7 (`.fvmrc`). The package SDK constraint is
  `^3.13.0`.
- Python 3.13 (uv) for the `tools/content_kit` rules and codegen.
- Django 5.2 for the one-field ripple into B04's authoring app.

**Primary Dependencies**:

- Engine: `content_models` (path, generated by B04) and `crypto ^3.0.7`. Dev: `test ^1.33.0`,
  `coverage ^1.16.0`, `lints ^6.1.0`.
- No Flutter, no plugins and no `build_runner` in the engine (R1).
- content_kit and the backend: no new dependencies.

**Storage**: None in the engine. Fixtures and `capabilities.json` are JSON files in the repo. The
backend gains one boolean column on `content_trip`, with an expand-only migration.

**Testing**:

- **Engine:** `package:test` unit tests per rules module, written first; the fixture replay,
  coverage and expectation suites; pacing-invariance and rebuild-at-cuts tests; a seeded fuzz with a
  money-conservation property; import, integer, clock and Arabic guards; and a replay perf budget.
  Coverage ≥ 90% is gated with `apps/mobile/tool/check_coverage.dart --min 90` (R16).
- **Python:** pytest for V-T8 to V-T11 and for fixture definitions.
- **Backend:** the existing pytest suite, against real Postgres and Valkey.

**Target Platform**: The Dart VM and AOT: Android and iOS through the app, and Linux in CI. Not Dart
web. The engine assumes 64-bit integers, although no value exceeds 2^53 (R3).

**Project Type**: A pure Dart library package inside the `wassal` monorepo, plus small changes to the
content tools, the app's tunables registry and B04's authoring app.

**Performance Goals**:

- Replaying the longest allowed trip (a full vehicle, 1,500 s, 1,200 commands) takes < 1 s on the
  CI runner (SC-004). The params' upper bounds fix that length (data-model.md "TripParams"). The
  expected figure is about 0.25 s.
- A single step costs well under 1 ms, leaving the trip scene its 33 ms.

**Constraints**:

- Deterministic: no clock, I/O or platform randomness (constitution I).
- Integer money and time only (II).
- Every number comes from `TripParams` (VIII).
- No text, only speech keys (VII, XII).
- A command log of at most 1,200 entries plus a final `Abandon`, under 64 KB (FR-055).
- The engine is immutable to callers.

**Scale/Scope**:

- 12 archetypes, 8 events, 13 commands and 4 boosters.
- About 45 domain events and 23 speech slots.
- 20 new tunables, for 50 in the registry.
- About 45 parity fixtures, including 5 sample-trip runs and 1 stress run.
- At most 30 passengers and 20 seats per trip (schema bounds).

**Locked stack.** Followed: pure Dart in the monorepo, pinned with FVM. `crypto` is a small pure-Dart
library from the Dart team, not a platform choice. M04 needs it anyway, to verify pack checksums.
Python stays on uv, and Django on 5.2.

**Security.**

- No secrets, no network and no personal data.
- Untrusted inputs (server-sent definitions, saved replay records) are validated before use: refusals
  at start, and rebuild refusals for malformed logs.
- Work is bounded: the schema caps passengers, seats and events; logs are capped at 1,200 commands (plus a final `Abandon`);
  the params' bounds cap a trip at 1,500 s of logic time, so a replay on the server (B08) is at most
  about 45,500 steps; stepping stops when the trip ends.
- The backend migration is additive. No security review is required by the constitution's list (no
  auth, purchases, ledger, sync, webhooks or personal data). The content-rule change is still
  reviewed with the PR.

**CI gates (constitution).**

- Mobile: `dart analyze` and the tests, now for the engine too, with its 90% gate.
- Parity: fixtures in Dart now, and in pytest from B05.
- Content: schema validation, `codegen --check` and the golden.
- The OpenAPI diff, which doesn't change.

**Gate result (pre-research): PASS.** One deviation from VIII, with its reason and its end, is in
Complexity Tracking, approved by the owner on 2026-10-20. Principles I to IV and VI have no
exceptions.

**Gate result (post-design): PASS.** The re-check after Phase 1 found no new issue:

- the contracts keep every rule number in `TripParams` and every default in `tunables.json`;
- the engine's public surface is read-only state plus commands;
- the import allowlist has no Flutter or plugin;
- every wire format is integer-only and canonical;
- the only constants are rule facts (rules.md §1.4).

**Structure decision.**

- **Where the engine goes.** It is the `packages/trip_engine` layout from mobile §5. `rules/` splits
  the rules by area, so each module maps to a section of `RULES.md` and to its own test file, and B05
  can mirror it file by file. `model/` holds only immutable data, so the rules never mutate state.
- **What else changes.** Outside the package, each change is the smallest one its contract needs:
  - one schema field and its codegen and authoring ripple;
  - one capabilities file and its rules;
  - the tunables registry and its defaults;
  - CI steps.
