## Integration

- **`integration_test/first_session_test.dart`** (offline, fresh database, `tutorialContent()`):
  1. Boot (outcome `offline`).
  2. Through the opening scene («يلا» ×3) and the Name card («تمام»).
  3. Each of the five trips, played by the coached player. Each lesson's step is seen, and the scene
     plays after trip 3.
  4. The fuel lesson before trip 5 fills the tank, then Home.
  5. Online against `FakeApi`: one `settings_update`, one `tutorial_gift`.

  Along the way it asserts SC-002: each in-trip lesson's step seen, the locked bar in all five trips,
  the scene once, the tank full once when trip 5 starts, and the end on Home.
- **`test/features/onboarding/first_session_kill_test.dart`:** a kill at each step (FR-051) with real
  SQLite and the trip records; the next launch's target is checked.
- **`test/backend/first_session_sync_test.dart`** (tag `backend`, `sync-e2e`): both operations
  against the dev stack; a filtered name comes back refused.
